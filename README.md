# 🇬🇭 Ghana GRA Individual Income Tax Calculator (2019 - 2026)
# About This Project
This Excel workbook helps individuals and sole proprietors in Ghana calculate their annual income tax based on the official Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) graduated tax rates. It covers 8 years of tax data, from 2019 to 2026, making it easy to compare how the tax bands have changed over time.
Whether you are an employee, a freelancer, or running your own business, this tool is built for you to assess your own tax (Self-assessment).

# What Is Inside
Cover Sheet - Project overview and navigation guide
Overview Sheet - Side by side comparison of all tax bands from 2019 to 2026
Year Selector - Enter your income once, pick a year, and get your tax calculated instantly
Individual Year Sheets (2019 - 2026) - A dedicated calculator for each year showing the full tax breakdown by band

# How to Use It
Download the Excel file
Open the Year Selector sheet
Type in your annual chargeable income
Enter the year you want to calculate for
Your total tax, effective tax rate, and net income are calculated automatically


# Data Source
All tax bands and rates are based on official GRA publications and amendments to the Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896), including Act 1007 (2020), Act 1071 (2022), Act 1093 (2023), and Act 1111 (2024).

# Important Note
This workbook is for educational and reference purposes only. Always confirm your figures with the Ghana Revenue Authority or a qualified tax professional before filing.
